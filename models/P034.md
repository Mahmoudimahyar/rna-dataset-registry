# RfamGen

P034 · Enabling RNA models and measurements

**Paper:** Deep generative design of RNA family sequences

https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-023-02148-8

**Task:** Generate functional RNA-family members, especially ribozymes.

**Method:** A variational autoencoder incorporates a covariance-model decoder; the CM formalizes alignment and paired structural constraints as a tree-structured probabilistic grammar.

**Inputs:** Family multiple sequence alignments and consensus secondary structure.

**Outputs:** New family-compatible RNA sequences sampled from latent space.
