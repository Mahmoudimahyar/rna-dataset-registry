# RiboNN + classic ML

P010 · Translation regulation and dynamics

**Paper:** Predicting the translation efficiency of messenger RNA in mammalian cells

https://doi.org/10.1038/s41587-025-02712-x

**Task:** Predict mammalian translation efficiency across cell types

**Method:** Multitask deep CNN captures position-dependent short sequence patterns; LGBM and lasso/elastic-net/random-forest baselines use lengths, codon/amino-acid/UTR features.

**Inputs:** Full transcript sequence/region labels; handcrafted features for classic models

**Outputs:** Cell-type-specific TE predictions
