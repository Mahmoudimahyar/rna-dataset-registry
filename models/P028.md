# RF and SeqNet

P028 · 5′UTR and translation initiation

**Paper:** Machine learning-based analysis of the impact of 5′ untranslated region on protein expression

https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkaf861

**Task:** Predict translation/protein expression from 5-prime UTR and reduce redundant experimental libraries.

**Method:** Compares feature-based random forests with a CNN-based SeqNet; clusters related sequences to reduce redundancy, and builds a smaller firefly-luciferase library to improve endpoint fidelity.

**Inputs:** 5-prime UTR sequence, sequence/secondary-structure features; reporter measurements.

**Outputs:** TE or luciferase-expression predictions and selected representative sequences.
