# RiboDecode

P032 · CDS and full mRNA design

**Paper:** Deep generative optimization of mRNA codon sequences for enhanced mRNA translation and therapeutic efficacy

https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-64894-x

**Task:** Predict and optimize translation while considering cellular environment and structure.

**Method:** Translation model has two convolutional and five fully connected layers. A learned differentiable MFE predictor supplies stability proxy; activation-maximization gradient ascent changes codon distributions while synonym regularization preserves protein identity.

**Inputs:** One-hot CDS, transcript abundance and RNA-seq-derived cellular-expression context.

**Outputs:** Translation score, MFE surrogate and synonymous optimized CDS.
