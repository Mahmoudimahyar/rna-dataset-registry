# BPfold

P043 · Enabling RNA models and measurements

**Paper:** Deep generalizable prediction of RNA secondary structure via base pair motif energy

https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-60048-1

**Task:** Predict secondary structure with better generalization to unseen RNA families.

**Method:** Builds an energy library for local base-pair motifs from de novo structural modeling; transformer blocks combine sequence and energy representations, followed by physical-constraint refinement.

**Inputs:** RNA sequence and base-pair motif-energy maps.

**Outputs:** Base-contact map refined into a secondary structure.
