# mRNA-LM

P008 · CDS and full mRNA design

**Paper:** mRNA-LM: full-length integrated SLM for mRNA analysis

https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkaf044

**Task:** Predict transcript stability, translation rate, transcript/protein expression

**Method:** Separate masked BERT models (codon tokens for CDS, bases for UTRs) are combined using CLIP-style contrastive alignment; LoRA fine-tunes a small subset plus prediction head.

**Inputs:** 5′ UTR, CDS and 3′ UTR

**Outputs:** Aligned segment embeddings and task-specific predictions
