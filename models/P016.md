# DIGs

P016 · CDS and full mRNA design

**Paper:** A generalized and efficient approach for complete mRNA design improves translation, stability and specificity

**Task:** Design UTR/CDS combinations and install cell-specific regulatory motifs

**Method:** Integrated gradients between opposite-performance sequences yields nucleotide preference arrays. Discrete design algorithms traverse these arrays; greedy motif placement incorporates RBP/miRNA regulation.

**Inputs:** Predictive model, high/low function reference sequences, motif constraints

**Outputs:** Attribution-guided RNA components/full transcripts
