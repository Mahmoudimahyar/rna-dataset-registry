# GEMORNA

P001 · CDS and full mRNA design

**Paper:** Deep generative models design mRNA sequences with enhanced translational capacity and stability

https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adr8470

**Task:** Generate CDS and UTRs with improved expression/stability

**Method:** Encoder-decoder Transformer translates protein into codon distributions; decoder-only UTR language models are pretrained and selectively fine-tuned. Autoregressive sampling/greedy/beam decoding generates candidates.

**Inputs:** Protein sequence for CDS; natural UTRs for UTR training

**Outputs:** Synonymous CDS and independently generated 5′/3′ UTR candidates
