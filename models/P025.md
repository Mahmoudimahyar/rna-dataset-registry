# CodonBERT

P025 · CDS and full mRNA design

**Paper:** CodonBERT large language model for mRNA vaccines

https://doi.org/10.1101/gr.278870.123

**Task:** Learn codon representations and predict mRNA properties such as expression and degradation.

**Method:** Twelve-block bidirectional transformer pretrained with masked-codon reconstruction and taxonomy-related sequence-pair prediction on over ten million mRNA sequences; adds trainable heads for downstream tasks.

**Inputs:** Codon-tokenized coding sequences.

**Outputs:** Embeddings or task-specific regression outputs after fine-tuning.
