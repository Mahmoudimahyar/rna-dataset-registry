# mDD-0

P011 · CDS and full mRNA design

**Paper:** mDD-0: mRNA Discrete Diﬀusion for Generation of Stable mRNA Sequences

**Task:** Generate species-conditioned full mRNA and stable 3′ UTR candidates

**Method:** Discrete denoising diffusion combines UTR modules, frozen ESM2 protein embeddings and a joint Transformer; Delphi-guided DPO/SVDD and synthetic-data fine-tuning target stability.

**Inputs:** Protein, species token, partially/fully masked UTRs

**Outputs:** CDS plus 5′/3′ UTR candidates
