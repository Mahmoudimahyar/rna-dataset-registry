# Delphi/LSTM predictor + design-build-test

P017 · Stability, 3′UTR and cell specificity

**Paper:** ML-driven design of 3’ UTRs for mRNA stability

https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.10.07.616676

**Task:** Predict mRNA half-life and design stable 3′ UTRs

**Method:** Compact convolution/LSTM predictor, CNN/Transformer/BERT comparisons; genetic algorithm, simulated annealing, mutagenesis, SeqProp and distribution-based design methods are experimentally compared.

**Inputs:** 3′ UTR sequence with measured half-life labels

**Outputs:** Half-life prediction and designed UTRs
