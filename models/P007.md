# Smart5UTR

P007 · 5′UTR and translation initiation

**Paper:** A novel deep generative model for mRNA vaccine development: Designing 5′ UTRs with N1-methyl-pseudouridine modification

https://doi.org/https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apsb.2023.11.003

**Task:** Generate high-MRL N1-methylpseudouridine-modified UTRs

**Method:** Multi-task autoencoder: CNN/dense encoder produces latent vector and MRL; decoder conditions on latent vector plus MRL. Reconstruction and MRL losses, including emphasis on high-MRL examples, guide training.

**Inputs:** One-hot 50-nt UTR and desired MRL

**Outputs:** Predicted MRL and generated UTR
