# G4mer

P039 · Enabling RNA models and measurements

**Paper:** G4mer: An RNA language model for transcriptome-wide identification of G-quadruplexes and disease variants from population-scale genetic data

https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-65020-7

**Task:** Predict RNA G-quadruplex formation/subtypes and effects of UTR variants.

**Method:** Fine-tunes a self-supervised mRNA BERT representation using experimental rG4-seeker regions and G-rich negatives, then interprets subtype sensitivity and flanking context.

**Inputs:** Transcript or variant sequence around candidate G-rich regions.

**Outputs:** rG4 probability/subtype and predicted mutational effect.
