# NuFold

P047 · Enabling RNA models and measurements

**Paper:** NuFold: end-to-end approach for RNA tertiary structure prediction with flexible nucleobase center representation

https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-56261-7

**Task:** Predict RNA tertiary structure from sequence.

**Method:** End-to-end neural structure prediction uses a flexible geometry representation, alignments including metagenomic information, and repeated recycling/refinement.

**Inputs:** RNA sequence plus constructed sequence alignments.

**Outputs:** Three-dimensional RNA conformation with flexible nucleobase-center representation.
