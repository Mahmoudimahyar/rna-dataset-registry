# PAL-AI

P041 · Stability, 3′UTR and cell specificity

**Paper:** PAL-AI reveals genetic determinants that control poly(A)-tail length during oocyte maturation, with relevance to human fertility

https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-62171-5

**Task:** Predict poly(A)-tail length changes during oocyte maturation and variant effects.

**Method:** Integrates convolutional and recurrent components, a simpler architecture related to Saluki. Learns CPE/PAS motifs and context; compares with a linear motif-based model.

**Inputs:** 3-prime UTR sequence, particularly regulatory elements near its end.

**Outputs:** Predicted maturation-dependent tail-length change.
