# OpenVaccine / Nullrecurrent, DegScore, DegScore-XGB

P052 · Stability, 3′UTR and cell specificity

**Paper:** Deep learning models for predicting RNA degradation via dual crowdsourcing

https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-022-00571-8

**Task:** Predict nucleotide hydrolysis to support stable mRNA design.

**Method:** Crowdsourced Eterna sequences and Kaggle model development. Winning architecture combines structure/graph-distance encodings, multihead attention and convolution; top approaches use pseudo-labels/ensembles. Simple sequence-window DegScore regression and boosted-tree versions are transparent baselines.

**Inputs:** RNA sequence plus one-/two-dimensional secondary-structure features.

**Outputs:** Per-linkage degradation/reactivity predictions and aggregate whole-RNA rate proxies.
