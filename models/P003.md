# PERSIST-seq, In-line-seq, DegScore, RiboTree

P003 · Stability, 3′UTR and cell specificity

**Paper:** Combinatorial optimization of mRNA structure, stability, and translation for RNA-based therapeutics

https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-28776-w

**Task:** Measure and optimize mRNA stability, structure and translation jointly

**Method:** PERSIST-seq measures cellular half-life/ribosome load; In-line-seq profiles hydrolysis. DegScore ridge regression uses local sequence/structure features; RiboTree uses Monte Carlo tree search for synonymous redesign.

**Inputs:** RNA sequence plus predicted secondary-structure neighborhood

**Outputs:** Per-nucleotide degradation score and candidate synonymous mRNAs
