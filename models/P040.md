# ERNIE-RNA

P040 · Enabling RNA models and measurements

**Paper:** ERNIE-RNA: An RNA Language Model with Structure-enhanced Representations

https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-64972-0

**Task:** Learn structural and functional RNA representations.

**Method:** Modified BERT integrates base-pairing restrictions/structural inductive bias during language-model pretraining rather than learning sequence context alone.

**Inputs:** RNA sequence with base-pairing priors in attention.

**Outputs:** Attention/embeddings and fine-tuned RNA property predictions.
