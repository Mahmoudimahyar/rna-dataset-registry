# Biophysical feature models plus LightGBM

P048 · Stability, 3′UTR and cell specificity

**Paper:** Predicting synthetic mRNA stability using massively parallel kinetic measurements, biophysical modeling, and machine learning

https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-54059-7

**Task:** Predict steady-state abundance and kinetic decay of synthetic bacterial mRNAs.

**Method:** Uses learn-by-design measurements and mechanistic isoform features, then trains LightGBM models for steady state and multiple kinetic timepoints; important variables include ribosome protection and RNase-related access.

**Inputs:** Sequence-derived transcription/translation/structure/RppH features and predicted isoforms.

**Outputs:** RNA abundance over time and decay-rate estimates.
