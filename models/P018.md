# UTR-LM

P018 · 5′UTR and translation initiation

**Paper:** A 5′ UTR language model for decoding untranslated regions of mRNA and function predictions

https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-024-00823-9

**Task:** Predict MRL, TE, expression, IRESs and guide UTR design

**Method:** Six-layer Transformer encoder pretrained with masked-base reconstruction plus structure/MFE supervision; task heads are fine-tuned for regression/classification.

**Inputs:** 5′ UTR sequence; computed structure and MFE in auxiliary training

**Outputs:** Embeddings, property predictions and selected UTR designs
