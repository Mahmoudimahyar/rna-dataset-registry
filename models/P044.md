# RNAmigos2

P044 · Enabling RNA models and measurements

**Paper:** RNAmigos2: accelerated structure-based RNA virtual screening with deep graph learning

https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-57852-0

**Task:** Rank small molecules for binding RNA pockets.

**Method:** Deep graph learning combines structural RNA representations, docking-derived synthetic data and experimental binding information to accelerate screening.

**Inputs:** Coarse-grained 3D RNA binding-site graph and ligand descriptors.

**Outputs:** Virtual-screening/binding ranking.
