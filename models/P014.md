# mRNArchitect

P014 · CDS and full mRNA design

**Paper:** mRNArchitect : sequence design of mRNA medicines

https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.12.03.626696

**Task:** Assemble manufacturable mRNAs and optimize design constraints

**Method:** Software assembles sequence modules and optimizes hard/soft constraints: species codons, U depletion, windowed GC, forbidden sites/repeats/hairpins. ViennaRNA supplies folding estimates. No learned AI model is established.

**Inputs:** Protein/CDS, chosen UTRs/poly(A), constraint settings

**Outputs:** Full mRNA plus CAI/MFE/composition statistics
