# SPOT-RNA

P035 · Enabling RNA models and measurements

**Paper:** RNA secondary structure prediction using an ensemble of two-dimensional deep neural networks and transfer learning

https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-13395-9

**Task:** Predict base-pairing including noncanonical and pseudoknot contacts.

**Method:** An ensemble of two-dimensional deep networks uses contextual sequence features, pretraining on more than 10,000 bpRNA structures, then transfer learning on fewer than 250 nonredundant high-resolution RNAs.

**Inputs:** RNA nucleotide sequence.

**Outputs:** Base-pair probabilities and predicted secondary structure.
