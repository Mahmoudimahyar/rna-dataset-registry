# SANDSTORM + GARDN

P012 · Enabling RNA models and measurements

**Paper:** Generative and predictive neural networks for the design of functional RNA molecules

https://doi.org/10.1101/2023.07.14.549043

**Task:** Predict and generate functional RNAs, including mRNA 5′ UTRs

**Method:** Compact dual-input CNN combines sequence and structure. A GAN generator learns realistic RNA designs and is guided by predictor outputs.

**Inputs:** RNA sequence and sequence-derived possible-base-pair compatibility arrays; not experimentally measured or thermodynamic pairing-probability structure labels.

**Outputs:** Function prediction; generated RNA designs
