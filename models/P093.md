# 3UTRBERT

P093 · Stability, 3′UTR and cell specificity

**Paper:** Deciphering 3′UTR Mediated Gene Regulation Using Interpretable Deep Representation Learning

https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.202407013

**Task:** Predict RBP binding, m6A sites, and mRNA subcellular localization.

**Method:** Masked-language pretraining of a 12-layer BERT encoder on human 3′UTRs; contiguous k-mer masking prevents trivial overlap leakage. Fine-tune short-window classifiers or freeze embeddings for longer localization inputs. Attention-derived candidate motifs are converted to PWMs and compared with known motifs.

**Inputs:** Human 3′UTR sequence windows, represented as overlapping k-mers.

**Outputs:** Binding/modification classifications or localization labels; not a half-life prediction or optimized therapeutic sequence.
