# CaLM

P036 · CDS and full mRNA design

**Paper:** Codon language embeddings provide strong signals for use in protein engineering

https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-024-00791-0

**Task:** Learn codon embeddings for protein/transcript property prediction.

**Method:** Masked-language-model pretraining learns codon sequence context, retaining synonymous information absent from amino-acid-only models; downstream models use those embeddings.

**Inputs:** Protein-coding DNA sequences tokenized as codons.

**Outputs:** Representations used for abundance, species, melting-point and engineering predictions.
