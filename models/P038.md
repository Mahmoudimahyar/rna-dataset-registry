# BiRNA-BERT

P038 · Enabling RNA models and measurements

**Paper:** BiRNA-BERT allows efficient RNA language modeling with adaptive tokenization

https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-025-08982-0

**Task:** Represent short or long RNAs with efficient tokenization.

**Method:** 117M-parameter transformer encoder pretrained on 36 million noncoding RNAs. Dual tokenization trades fine nucleotide resolution against sequence compression for longer contexts.

**Inputs:** RNA sequence with adaptive nucleotide-level or byte-pair tokenization.

**Outputs:** Embeddings and fine-tuned classification/structural predictions.
