# LinearDesign

P005 · CDS and full mRNA design

**Paper:** Algorithm for optimized mRNA design improves stability and immunogenicity

https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06127-z

**Task:** Optimize synonymous CDS for folding energy and codon usage

**Method:** A weighted deterministic finite-state automaton compactly represents synonymous sequences; lattice parsing/RNA-folding DP optimizes energy plus log codon usage. Beam search offers approximate speedups. This is algorithmic optimization, not a trained neural model.

**Inputs:** Protein and species codon-usage table; tradeoff λ

**Outputs:** CDS plus secondary structure, MFE and CAI
