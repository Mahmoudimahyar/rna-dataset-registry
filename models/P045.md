# Ninetails

P045 · Enabling RNA models and measurements

**Paper:** Direct profiling of non-adenosines in poly(A) tails of endogenous and therapeutic mRNAs with Ninetails

https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-57787-6

**Task:** Detect and quantify non-adenosine residues in poly(A) tails.

**Method:** Converts signal chunks to Gramian angular images and classifies them with a small VGG-like CNN. Synthetic decorated tails provide known labels, followed by biological and therapeutic-mRNA applications.

**Inputs:** Nanopore direct-RNA-sequencing current signals from tail regions.

**Outputs:** A/C/G/U signal-class assignments and tail-composition estimates.
