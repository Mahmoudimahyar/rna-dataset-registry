# UTR-seq sequence/kinetic regression models

P022 · Stability, 3′UTR and cell specificity

**Paper:** A Massively Parallel Reporter Assay of 3′ UTR Sequences Identifies In Vivo Rules for mRNA Degradation

https://doi.org/10.1016/j.molcel.2017.11.014

**Task:** Predict early versus late mRNA degradation during zebrafish embryogenesis and design regulatory UTRs.

**Method:** Fits first-order decay models with constant or step-changing rates; uses likelihood-ratio model selection and k-mer/sequence-based regression. Identifies six classes of stabilizing/destabilizing sequence signals.

**Inputs:** Reporter 3-prime UTR sequences and time-series RNA abundance, with polyadenylation conditions.

**Outputs:** Decay classes, early/late rate parameters and predicted RNA trajectories.
