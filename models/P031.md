# mRNABERT

P031 · CDS and full mRNA design

**Paper:** mRNABERT: advancing mRNA sequence design with a universal language model and comprehensive dataset

https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-65340-8

**Task:** Provide integrated representations for UTR/CDS and full-mRNA prediction/design tasks.

**Method:** Twelve-transformer-block masked language model; dual tokenization retains nucleotide resolution in UTRs and codon semantics in CDSs. Cross-modality contrastive learning aligns CDS and protein representations.

**Inputs:** Full mRNA with region-aware nucleotide/codon tokenization, plus paired proteins during contrastive training.

**Outputs:** Embeddings, RBP-site/property predictions and task-specific design scores.
