# RiboTIE

P046 · Translation regulation and dynamics

**Paper:** Deep learning to decode sites of RNA translation in normal and cancerous tissues

https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-56543-0

**Task:** Detect translated ORFs from ribosome profiling.

**Method:** Transformer models variable-length count profiles, with pretrained models and adaptation across experiments, avoiding reliance only on manually chosen periodicity summaries.

**Inputs:** Mapped ribosome-footprint counts along transcripts.

**Outputs:** Translation initiation/translated ORF calls.
