# RhoFold+

P033 · Enabling RNA models and measurements

**Paper:** Accurate RNA 3D structure prediction using a language model-based deep learning approach

https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-024-02487-0

**Task:** Predict RNA tertiary structure; indirect relevance to mRNA design.

**Method:** Uses a language model pretrained on approximately 23.7 million RNAs together with deep structural modeling; alignment search and refinement contribute structural information.

**Inputs:** Single-chain RNA sequence, RNA-FM representation and alignment information.

**Outputs:** Three-dimensional atomic RNA structure and structural confidence/features.
