# RNop

P013 · CDS and full mRNA design

**Paper:** A New Deep-learning-Based Approach For mRNA Optimization: High Fidelity, Computation Efficiency, and Multiple Optimization Factors

https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2505.23862

**Task:** Optimize synonymous CDS fidelity, codon/tRNA adaptation and folding proxy

**Method:** Codon tokenization and image-like reshaping feed a Vision-Transformer-style sequence-to-sequence model. GPLoss penalizes amino-acid changes; CAI/tAI losses target codon preferences; learned MFE loss models provide structural proxy.

**Inputs:** CDS plus target-species preferences

**Outputs:** Optimized CDS
