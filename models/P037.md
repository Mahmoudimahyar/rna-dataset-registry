# Benchmark of eleven genomic language models

P037 · Enabling RNA models and measurements

**Paper:** Benchmarking pre-trained genomic language models for RNA sequence-related predictive applications

https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-66899-y

**Task:** Determine which pretrained model helps particular RNA prediction tasks.

**Method:** Standardizes evaluation of genomic language models versus task-specific alternatives; examines tokenization, pretraining corpus, input length and limited-data regimes.

**Inputs:** RNA sequences and labels for ncRNA class, m6A, splice sites and translation efficiency.

**Outputs:** Comparative task performance under changing data size, imbalance and context.
