# RNAdegformer

P021 · Stability, 3′UTR and cell specificity

**Paper:** RNAdegformer: accurate prediction of mRNA degradation at nucleotide resolution with deep learning

https://doi.org/10.1093/bib/bbac581

**Task:** Predict chemical degradation at nucleotide resolution.

**Method:** Combines local convolutional sequence features and global self-attention with biophysical structure features; uses supervised and auxiliary training. It is a predictor, so candidate design requires an external search procedure.

**Inputs:** RNA sequence, predicted secondary structure/loop types and base-pair probabilities.

**Outputs:** Per-position reactivity/degradation measurements under specified solution conditions.
