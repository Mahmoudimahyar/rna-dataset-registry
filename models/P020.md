# mRNA2vec

P020 · CDS and full mRNA design

**Paper:** mRNA2vec: mRNA Embedding with Language Model in the 5'UTR-CDS for mRNA Design

**Task:** Predict translation/expression, CDS stability and protein production

**Method:** Data2vec teacher-student contextual target learning; position-dependent masking emphasizes translation-relevant regions; auxiliary MFE and structure tasks. Frozen encoder supports a small trained regressor.

**Inputs:** Joint 5′ UTR/CDS triplet tokens

**Outputs:** Context embeddings and downstream predictions
