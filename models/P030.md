# codonGPT

P030 · CDS and full mRNA design

**Paper:** codonGPT: reinforcement learning on a generative language model enables scalable mRNA design

https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkaf1345

**Task:** Generate protein-preserving CDSs and optimize surrogate expression/stability/composition rewards.

**Method:** GPT-2-style codon model trained on 338,417 CDSs; inference-time pre-softmax masking restricts each position to synonyms. REINFORCE adapts a protein-specific policy to multi-objective reward.

**Inputs:** Fixed target amino-acid sequence and codon history.

**Outputs:** Autoregressively generated synonymous CDS candidates.
