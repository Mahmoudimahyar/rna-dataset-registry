# LinearDesign2

P023 · CDS and full mRNA design

**Paper:** Joint Design of 5' Untranslated Region and Coding Sequence of mRNA

https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2410.20781

**Task:** Co-design UTR and synonymous coding sequence for translation-initiation, codon preference and structure tradeoffs.

**Method:** Uses a five-layer CNN TIE predictor on UTR plus first 30 CDS nucleotides and handcrafted features. Alternates evolutionary UTR optimization conditional on CDS and CDS optimization conditional on UTR; integrates predicted TIE with CAI/MFE.

**Inputs:** Fixed protein sequence, candidate 5-prime UTR/CDS, sequence features.

**Outputs:** A jointly optimized UTR/CDS sequence with TIE, CAI and MFE scores.
