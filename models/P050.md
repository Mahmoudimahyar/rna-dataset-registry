# mRNAid

P050 · CDS and full mRNA design

**Paper:** mRNAid, an open-source platform for therapeutic mRNA design and optimization strategies

https://doi.org/10.1093/nargab/lqae028

**Task:** Design and visualize mRNAs satisfying sequence/structure objectives.

**Method:** Builds on DNA Chisel; hard constraints must hold and soft objectives are weighted for optimization. It integrates configurable design rules rather than learning one new AI predictor.

**Inputs:** Protein/CDS and user-selected codon, composition, motif and structure requirements.

**Outputs:** Constraint-satisfying synonymous sequences and property reports.
