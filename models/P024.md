# BiLSTM-CRF codon-box model

P024 · CDS and full mRNA design

**Paper:** Codon optimization with deep learning to enhance protein expression

https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-74091-z

**Task:** Choose synonymous codons to improve recombinant protein expression in E. coli.

**Method:** Recasts codon optimization as sequence annotation, learns context with bidirectional LSTM and dependency structure with conditional random field rather than selecting each codon independently.

**Inputs:** Amino-acid sequence represented for sequence labeling and E. coli training coding sequences.

**Outputs:** Codon-box annotations converted into synonymous DNA/CDS designs.
