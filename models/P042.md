# RiNALMo

P042 · Enabling RNA models and measurements

**Paper:** RiNALMo: general-purpose RNA language models can generalize well on structure prediction tasks

https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-60872-5

**Task:** Learn general RNA representations and improve structure generalization.

**Method:** 650M-parameter language model pretrained on 36 million noncoding RNAs using masked-token learning; downstream fine-tuning learns structural heads.

**Inputs:** RNA nucleotide sequence.

**Outputs:** Embeddings and task-specific structural/property predictions.
